# Hints

These hints are meant as nudges before using the full walkthrough.

**Spoiler warning:** early sections stay broad, but later hints name major objects, areas, and puzzle shapes.

## Before Looking Anything Up

- Save often. The wizard can waste time, move objects, or interrupt a plan.
- Keep a map, and mark rooms where directions behave strangely.
- Keep a staging area for treasures and objects you may need later.
- Read room descriptions for colors, shapes, containers, and mechanical details.
- When a command fails, simplify it before trying a more elaborate sentence.

## Getting Started

<details>
<summary>What should I take first?</summary>

Start with the familiar adventuring tools near the barrow. You will want light and protection before exploring deep underground.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Where should I go after the opening?</summary>

Move south through the early rooms until you reach the garden area. The gazebo contains an object that helps with a later vertical puzzle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Why does the wizard keep disrupting me?</summary>

That is part of Zork II. Save periodically, keep important items organized, and wait out temporary effects when needed.

</details>

## Carousel And Riddle Area

<details>
<summary>The Carousel Room is confusing.</summary>

It is meant to be. Until you change something elsewhere, movement from the carousel can send you somewhere unexpected. Keep returning to the carousel and trying exits until you reach useful neighboring rooms.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I stop the Carousel?</summary>

Look for a non-human helper and a control room. The controls are unsafe for you, but not necessarily unsafe for something you can command.

</details>

<details>
<summary>The stone door has a riddle.</summary>

Think of something deep, old, and associated with water. You can answer with a short phrase.

</details>

## Well, Cakes, And Robot

<details>
<summary>How does the bucket work?</summary>

It reacts to water. Bring water in a container, get into the bucket, and change the bucket's load.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What are the cakes for?</summary>

Their colors matter. One changes your size, one reverses that change, and one is useful somewhere wet.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I use the robot?</summary>

Talk to it directly. Commands of the form `tell robot "go east"` and `tell robot "push triangle"` are the right idea.

</details>

## Dragon And Princess

<details>
<summary>Can I simply kill the dragon?</summary>

Not directly. You can provoke it, lead it, and let the environment solve the problem.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What should I do with the princess?</summary>

Do not attack her or abandon her. Follow her, wait when she pauses, and pay attention to where she wants to go.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I get the key from the unicorn?</summary>

The unicorn trusts someone else more than it trusts you. Let that scene develop instead of forcing it.

</details>

## Bank, Door, And Balloon

<details>
<summary>The bank seems impossible to leave with valuables.</summary>

The curtain of light changes where you are. Use the walls and curtain to move valuables without triggering the obvious route out.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I get the key behind the tiny-room door?</summary>

Use something flat under the door and something thin through the keyhole. Then check what fell.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I control the balloon?</summary>

Heat makes it rise; removing heat makes it descend. When you land at a ledge, secure the basket before getting out.

</details>

## Endgame

<details>
<summary>What are the colored spheres for?</summary>

Look for matching stands. The colors are a strong clue, and one additional sphere is hidden behind a dangerous-looking obstacle.

</details>

<details>
<summary>What does the demon want?</summary>

Payment. Bring treasures in batches and keep giving until the demon accepts the fee.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I use the wand?</summary>

Use it on a large obstacle whose movement would reveal a new route. The magic word you need is tied to what the wand should make the obstacle do.

</details>

<details>
<summary>How do I pass the dog?</summary>

Find something that belongs on a dog, then use it before trying to pass.

</details>
