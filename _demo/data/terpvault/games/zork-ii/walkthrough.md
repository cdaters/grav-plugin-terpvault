# Zork II Walkthrough

**Full spoiler warning:** this is a complete command route for Zork II. Use the hints first if you only want nudges.

This route reaches the end with a final score of 400/400 in 372 moves and the rank Master Adventurer.

## Route Notes

The Bank of Zork section preserves both Bank treasures without using the Zurich gnome. Drop the portrait in the Safety Depository, recover the bills from the Vault, drop the bills, go west twice back to the Safety Depository, take both the bills and portrait, enter the curtain, and exit south. This lets the demon accept the portrait as the ninth counted treasure and the stack of zorkmid bills as the tenth.
