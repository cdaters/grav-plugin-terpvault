# How To Play Zork II

Zork II is a parser adventure. Type a short command at the prompt, then press Enter. The game describes where you are, what you can see, and what changes after each command.

## Useful Commands

- `look` repeats the current room description.
- `inventory` lists what you are carrying.
- `examine lamp` looks closely at an object.
- `take sword` picks up an object.
- `drop teapot` leaves an object behind.
- `read book` or `read label` checks written clues.
- `open door`, `unlock door with key`, and `put object in container` handle common puzzle actions.
- `wait` lets time pass when a scene or mechanism needs a moment.

Movement is usually by compass direction: `north`, `south`, `east`, `west`, `northeast`, `northwest`, `southeast`, `southwest`, `up`, and `down`. Short forms such as `n`, `s`, `e`, `w`, `ne`, and `d` usually work.

## Staying Alive

Keep track of your lamp and avoid wandering in darkness. Save before risky experiments, before entering strange machinery, and before trying commands around characters or obvious hazards.

Use the story commands for saves:

```text
save
restore
```

## Play Advice

Make a map. Zork II includes areas where directions can be unreliable or temporarily confusing, so write down room names and how you returned to familiar ground.

Do not carry every treasure forever. A safe staging area can save time when the wizard interferes or when inventory weight becomes a problem.

When a puzzle has a strange object, a suspicious room description, or a repeated color/shape clue, try simple experiments first. `look`, `examine`, `read`, `put`, `give`, `tell`, and `wait` are often enough.

Use `hints.md` for progressive nudges. Use `walkthrough.md` only when you want a complete spoiler route.
